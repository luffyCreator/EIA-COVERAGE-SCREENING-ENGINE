  • Presidential Proclamation No. 2146 — Environmentally Critical Projects and Areas (14 Dec 1981)
  • Presidential Proclamation No. 803 — Declaring Golf Course Projects as Environmentally Critical (1996)
  • DENR Administrative Order 2003-30 — Implementing Rules and Regulations of the PEIS System
  • DENR-EMB Memorandum Circular 2007-002 — EIA Revised Procedural Manual for DAO 2003-30
  • EMB Memorandum Circular 005, series 2014 — Revised Guidelines for Coverage Screening and Standardized Requirements (Annex A: Project Thresholds · Annex B: Modification Decision Chart · Annex C: Project Description pro-forma) — the primary source for every threshold in this tool

  • DAO 2026-09 — Enhanced ECC guidelines for Offshore Wind (supersedes DAO 2024-02); DAO 2025-25 — CDRA requirement for OSW and FPV EIS
  • DAO 2025-36, as amended by DAO 2026-23 — Streamlined ECC/CNC processing for Boracay Island
  • MC 2021-12 (steel as metal industries), MC 2022-06 (resorts/tourism thresholds), MC 2024-003 (water supply thresholds), MC 2020-27 (non-metallic extraction) — amend parts of Annex A; flagged on the affected results
  • MC 2020-31 (ECC 5-year validity); MC 2021-05 (minor amendments); DAO 2016-28 (fees); MC 2015-008 / 2022-002 (ECC Online, IEE Checklist)
  • DAO 2017-15 (public participation) as amended; DAO 2018-14 (reclamation area clearance) as amended; DMO 2025-01 (protected areas)
